## Key Observations
1. **Zero remotely exploitable findings**: All leads require either local access, user interaction (extension/browser install), or active testing with accounts.
2. **No passive proof**: The probe-results.md shows no HTTP responses confirming any vulnerability. All binary acquisition channels are blocked (CloudFront DNS failures, 403/404 on mirrors).
3. **Speculative nature**: Most leads are based on the existence of Whale-only code paths (custom prefs, forked libraries) without evidence of actual insecurity.
4. **Past CVEs fixed**: The sidebar, dual-tab, and extension XSS leads reference CVEs that were already fixed. Claims of recurrence/new variants are unverified.
5. **Scope boundary**: Several leads blur the line between client-side (in scope) and server-side (*.naver.com, out of scope). Lead 10 is clearly out of scope.
6. **Model convergence**: All 4 active models converged on the same ~9 hypotheses, suggesting they're all working from the same limited information (vendor docs, NVD, binary strings) rather than discovering independent findings.
